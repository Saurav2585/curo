# Curo — Commercial Strategy (India)

*A monetisation blueprint for turning Curo into a commercially viable healthcare SaaS marketplace in India. Strategy only — no implementation. All prices are startup-stage recommendations to validate with real customers, not fixed decisions.*

Prepared as: CPO · Healthcare SaaS Consultant · Pricing Strategist · Growth PM · Conversion & UX Strategist.

---

## 0. The strategic wedge (why anyone switches to a new platform)

Curo cannot out-spend Practo, Apollo, or Tata 1mg. It wins on **one sharp promise the incumbents break: a booking that is actually confirmed, on a calendar that is actually the doctor's.** Every pricing decision below protects that promise.

Two positioning choices follow directly, and they are the backbone of the whole model:

1. **Zero commission on consultations for providers.** Practo stacks a per-doctor subscription **plus** listing fees **plus** a cut of every consultation, so a provider's cost rises with their own success. Curo charges a **flat SaaS subscription and takes nothing per booking.** This is the single most compelling reason a doctor switches, and it makes revenue predictable for both sides. *(Benchmark: Practo Ray is per-doctor/month layered with commission — see sources.)*
2. **Trust is never for sale.** In healthcare, selling "verified" badges or letting paid placement bury better-rated doctors is a safety and reputation risk. Curo keeps **verification free and mandatory**, and any paid visibility is clearly labelled and capped. This is both ethical and, long-term, a marketing asset ("the platform you can trust").

## 1. Revenue model overview

Four streams, sequenced by how soon they should ship:

| Stream | Who pays | When to build | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Provider SaaS subscriptions** | Doctors, clinics, hospitals | First | The core, predictable revenue. Flat fee, zero commission. |
| **Patient membership (optional)** | Patients who want extras | Second | Never gates core booking; adds convenience + savings. |
| **Visibility upsells** | Providers | Third | Featured/sponsored placement, clearly labelled. |
| **Value-added credits** | Providers | Later | SMS credits, extra reception seats, multi-branch. |

The marketplace stays **free to use** for any patient and free to list (at a basic tier) for any verified doctor. Revenue comes from depth, not from tolls on the core action.

## 2. Patient membership — "Curo Plus" (optional, never required)

The platform is fully usable free, forever. Membership sells **convenience and savings**, not access.

| | **Free** (default) | **Curo Plus** | **Curo Plus Family** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | ₹0 | **₹149/mo** or **₹1,199/yr** | **₹399/mo** or **₹3,499/yr** |
| Book any doctor | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Booking reminders (email) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| SMS reminders | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Family profiles | 1 | up to 4 | up to 6 |
| Priority booking window (early access to newly opened slots) | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Partner-clinic consultation discount | — | up to 10% | up to 15% |
| Partner lab-test discount | — | — | up to 20% |
| Health record vault | basic | ✓ | ✓ |
| Priority human support | — | ✓ | ✓ |

**Design rules:**
- **Optional and invisible until relevant.** A patient never hits a paywall to book. Plus is offered *after* value is felt (post-booking), never before.
- **Savings must exceed the fee.** For a family that books 4–6 visits a year, the discounts alone should out-earn the subscription — the plan pays for itself, which is the only honest reason to renew.
- **Renewal strategy:** annual plans auto-remind 7 days before renewal with a plain "here's what you saved this year: ₹X" statement. Renewal is earned by demonstrated value, not by dark patterns. Easy cancel, always visible.

**Upgrade triggers (helpful, not aggressive):**
- After a completed booking: "Add SMS reminders and skip the queue for ₹149/mo."
- When adding a 2nd family member: "Manage the whole family on one account with Plus."
- When viewing a partner clinic: "Plus members save 10% here."

## 3. Provider subscriptions — "Curo for Providers" (never shown to patients)

Flat SaaS, **zero commission**. This is the primary revenue engine.

| | **Solo — Free** | **Solo — Pro** | **Clinic** | **Hospital** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly | ₹0 | **₹899/mo** | **₹2,999/mo** | Custom |
| Yearly (2 months free) | ₹0 | **₹8,990/yr** | **₹29,990/yr** | Custom |
| Doctors included | 1 | 1 | up to 10 | unlimited |
| Live slot booking | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Public profile | basic | enhanced | enhanced | enhanced |
| Schedule + appointments | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Commission per booking | **0%** | **0%** | **0%** | **0%** |
| Booking analytics | — | ✓ | ✓ (per-doctor + clinic) | ✓ + exports |
| SMS/WhatsApp confirmations | pay-as-you-go | included allowance | larger allowance | custom |
| Reception staff seats | — | 1 | up to 3 (add more) | custom |
| Multi-branch | — | — | 1 branch | multiple |
| Priority support | community | email | priority | dedicated CSM + onboarding |

**Free trial:** 30 days of Pro/Clinic, no card required. The trial is the acquisition engine — a doctor experiences the full product, and the zero-commission promise means there's no hidden cost to fear.

**Renewal & upgrade path:**
- **Free → Pro:** triggered when a solo doctor hits real usage — e.g. wants analytics, or their profile is getting views they could convert. The prompt is contextual, inside the dashboard, framed as "unlock what your demand is already generating."
- **Pro → Clinic:** when they add a **second doctor** — the seat limit is the natural, honest upgrade moment.
- **Clinic → Hospital:** when they add an **11th doctor** or a **second branch**.
- Annual billing is the default nudge (2 months free), presented honestly, never forced.

**Why these numbers:** they sit meaningfully below the *total* cost of an incumbent (subscription + listing + commission), so the switching pitch is "predictable and cheaper, and you keep 100% of every consultation fee." For a Pro doctor seeing even 30 paid consults/month, the ₹899 fee is a rounding error against the commission they'd otherwise lose.

## 4. Additional revenue — visibility & value-added

Recommended (ethical, healthcare-appropriate):

| Upsell | Verdict | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Featured doctor** (top of a specialty/city, labelled "Featured") | ✅ Include | Standard, honest, clearly disclosed. Cap the number per specialty so results stay useful. |
| **Sponsored/priority search placement** | ✅ Include, with guardrails | Must be labelled "Sponsored" and never override a strong ratings/quality floor. Safety first. |
| **Homepage promotion** (seasonal health campaigns) | ✅ Include | Works as tasteful, relevant placement. |
| **Analytics add-on** (deeper insights) | ✅ Include | Natural expansion revenue; bundle basics in Pro, sell depth. |
| **Reception staff seats** | ✅ Include | Real operational need for clinics; priced per seat. |
| **Multi-branch support** | ✅ Include | Clear hospital/chain value. |
| **SMS / WhatsApp credits** | ✅ Include | Pay-as-you-go with allowances in paid tiers. |
| **Verified badge** | ❌ Do **not** sell | Verification must be free and mandatory. Selling trust corrupts the platform's core promise and is a patient-safety risk. |

**Guardrail principle:** paid visibility can influence *order*, never *legitimacy*. A sponsored doctor is still a verified doctor; a non-paying doctor is never hidden or demoted below a usable threshold.

## 5. Landing-page strategy (public sees patient offers only)

**Rule: provider pricing never appears on the public patient site.** Patients seeing "clinics pay ₹2,999/mo" erodes the feeling of a neutral, patient-first marketplace.

- The **public pricing page** shows **only Curo Plus** (patient membership) alongside the always-free booking.
- **"For Doctors / Clinics"** in the header/footer leads to a **dedicated provider marketing page** (`/for-providers`) — a separate narrative: zero commission, live schedule ownership, verified patients, predictable cost. This page sells the *value*; it can show indicative provider pricing **or** route to "start your 30-day trial" / "talk to us."
- **Business pricing is best revealed in-context:** during provider onboarding and inside the provider dashboard, where it's relevant and private — not on a page a patient browses.
- Provider marketing runs on its own channels: search ads for "clinic software India / zero commission booking," medical-association partnerships, direct outbound to clinics, and referral from already-onboarded doctors.

## 6. Upgrade touchpoints (contextual, helpful, never nagging)

| Audience | Where | Message frame |
|---|---|---|
| **Patient** | After a booking; when adding family; on a partner-clinic profile | "Here's a convenience/saving relevant to what you just did." |
| **Solo doctor** | Dashboard when viewing analytics they don't have; when profile views spike | "Your demand is already here — unlock the tools to convert it." |
| **Clinic** | When adding the 2nd/11th doctor; when reception needs a login | "You've outgrown this tier — here's the natural next step." |
| **Hospital** | When adding a branch; at data-export limits | "Let's set up multi-branch / talk to our team." |

**Frequency rule:** at most one contextual prompt per session, dismissible, and it never blocks the task. The test: *would a helpful colleague mention this here?* If not, don't show it.

## 7. Promotional strategy (ethical, healthcare-appropriate)

**Avoid:** countdown timers, fake "only 2 left," artificial urgency, health-anxiety framing. In healthcare these are both distasteful and a trust risk.

**Recommended launch promotions:**
- **Founding Provider offer:** the first 100 clinics/doctors get an extended trial (e.g. 90 days) or their first year at a founding rate, locked for 2 years. Real scarcity (genuinely limited cohort), honestly framed.
- **Annual = 2 months free** — a permanent, transparent incentive, not a fake sale.
- **Provider referral:** a doctor who refers another verified provider earns account credit. Growth that compounds trust (doctors vouch for doctors).
- **Patient referral:** invite a family member, both get a month of Plus. Ties naturally to the family-profiles benefit.
- **"You saved ₹X" renewal statements** for Plus members — value-based retention instead of urgency.
- **Seasonal health campaigns** (e.g. monsoon/flu check-ups) as relevant, non-manipulative homepage promotion.

## 8. Dashboard upgrade strategy (provider side)

The dashboard is where provider expansion revenue is earned. Principles:
- **Show the value before the ask.** A locked analytics chart shows a blurred preview with "See your booking trends with Pro" — the doctor sees what they're missing, framed as their own data.
- **Tie every prompt to a real limit or a real signal** (seat reached, profile views high, SMS allowance low). Never a generic "Upgrade now!" banner.
- **A single, quiet "Plan" area** in settings with a transparent comparison and one-click upgrade — no maze, easy downgrade, honest proration.
- **Expansion, not extraction:** the goal is for a provider to upgrade because they grew, so their success and Curo's revenue rise together (net-revenue-retention thinking).

## 9. Revenue model — how it compounds

- **Predictable base:** provider subscriptions (MRR/ARR) — the stable core, unaffected by booking volume swings.
- **Volume-linked but ethical:** SMS/WhatsApp credits and reception seats scale with a clinic's real activity.
- **Marketplace liquidity flywheel:** free booking + zero commission → more doctors list → more availability → more patients → more doctors want in. Membership and visibility monetise the liquidity without taxing the core loop.
- **Patient membership** is a margin layer that also deepens retention (families with saved profiles and health records don't churn).

The deliberate choice to forgo per-consultation commission trades some short-term take-rate for a **much stronger acquisition story and cleaner unit economics** — the classic wedge a challenger uses against an incumbent that over-monetises.

## 10. Future expansion opportunities

Sequenced, each building on the trusted-booking core:
1. **Tel«consultation** (video) as a Plus benefit and a provider add-on — the obvious adjacent surface.
2. **Lab-test booking** with partner discounts (already hinted in the Plus tier) — a second marketplace on the same trust rails.
3. **e-Prescriptions & records** — deepens the patient vault and provider workflow lock-in.
4. **Insurance / cashless tie-ups** — where Zocdoc stumbles (insurance-first friction), Curo can add it *after* the booking is already easy.
5. **Corporate / employer health plans** — bulk Plus memberships sold B2B2C.
6. **Clinic operations suite** — billing, inventory, staff — expanding provider ARPU once the booking relationship is trusted.
7. **Regional expansion** — the zero-commission wedge travels to other emerging markets with the same incumbent over-monetisation problem.

---

## Guardrails to carry into implementation

- Core booking is **always free** for patients and **always available** at a free provider tier.
- **Verification is free and mandatory** — never a paid upsell.
- Paid visibility is **labelled and capped**; ratings/safety floors are never overridden by spend.
- **No dark patterns** — no fake urgency, easy cancellation, honest renewal, transparent proration.
- **Provider pricing stays off the patient-facing site.**
